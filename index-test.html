Packrafting – an Exciting Adventure

Packrafting combines outdoor activity, exploration of nature, and water travel. Its core is a lightweight, compact, and versatile inflatable boat—a packraft—that fits easily in your backpack.

Advantages of Packrafting

  • Compact and portable: packrafts weigh from only 2 to 5 kg.
  • Versatile: suitable for rivers, lakes, and seas.
  • Eco-friendly: requires no fuel and does not pollute nature.
  • Accessible: suitable for everyone, regardless of skill level.

Where Can You Go Packrafting?

Israel is a fantastic place for packrafting. Its rivers, lakes, and coastlines offer a variety of landscapes:

  • Rivers: For example, Jordan and Tini.
  • Lakes: Kinneret (Sea of Galilee).
  • Coastlines: Mediterranean and Red Sea.

How to Get Started?

The minimum kit for packrafting includes:

  • Packraft – light and durable.
  • Paddle – collapsible and lightweight.
  • Life vest – for safety.
  • Dry bag – to protect your belongings from water.
  • Gear – comfortable clothing and shoes.

Conclusion

Packrafting is not just a sport—it’s a lifestyle. It’s a way to explore nature, escape the city, and challenge yourself. Try it and discover a new world of adventure!
